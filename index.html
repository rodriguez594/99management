About

99MGMT

99MGMT is the management company under 99. We work with a small roster of artists, so each one gets proper attention.

We take care of the business side: management, distribution, promotion and partnerships. Video and music production is handled by our sister company 99ENT.

Services

  1. 01

    Artist Management

    Day-to-day management, career planning and long-term strategy.

  2. 02

    Distribution

    Releases delivered to streaming services and stores, with registration and metadata handled.

  3. 03

    Promotion

    Release campaigns, playlist pitching, press and social media.

  4. 04

    Partnerships & Bookings

    Brand deals, concerts and appearances.
