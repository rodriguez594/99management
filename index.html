01 — About

We build careers, not campaigns.

99MGMT is the artist management arm of 99. We represent a focused roster of artists and keep it small on purpose, so every name gets the attention, strategy and network it deserves.

We handle the business side of a career — management, distribution and promotion — so our artists can focus on the music.
