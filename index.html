01 — About

We build careers, not campaigns.

99MGMT is an independent management agency representing a focused roster of artists and creators. We keep the roster small on purpose, so every name gets the attention, strategy and network it deserves.

From first release to sold-out shows, from brand deals to long-term partnerships — we handle the business so our talent can focus on the work.
